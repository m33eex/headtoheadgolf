HeadToHeadGolf — Account Deletion Information

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. How to request deletion

In the HeadToHeadGolf app, open your profile/account settings and select the account deletion option, then follow the confirmation steps. If you cannot access the app, email h2hgolf@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We may need to verify your identity.

2. Ownership responsibilities

If you are responsible for competitions or have pending ownership transfers, you may need to resolve those responsibilities before account deletion can be completed. Contact support if you need assistance.

3. What is removed and what may remain

When deletion completes successfully, the service is designed to remove your login/authentication account, unlink your user ID from competition player records and delete account profile photographs stored in the account’s profile-photo folder.

Organiser-entered player names, email addresses, handicaps, scores, results and statistics may remain where a lawful basis justifies retaining historical competition records. Such records may still identify you. You may request review, correction or erasure separately. Some information may persist temporarily in backups or be retained for legal, security or dispute-resolution purposes.

4. Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through Apple. Cancel separately in Apple subscription settings.

5. Help and privacy rights

For deletion problems or questions about retained information, contact h2hgolf@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy for your data-protection rights.
