1. What this policy covers

HeadToHeadGolf is a golf competition management app for organisers, players and spectators. This policy describes personal information processed when people register, join or run competitions, record scores, upload profile photographs and use support or subscription features.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use the app, this may include: account identifier, name, email address, profile photo, competition memberships and roles, golf handicap and player roster information, scores and match results, competition history, uploaded side-competition photos, support correspondence, subscription status and Apple transaction identifiers, and technical security or diagnostic records. Verify against the production database, SDKs and analytics configuration before publication.

Organisers may enter other players' names, handicaps and competition details. Organisers should ensure they have a suitable basis to share that information and explain how the event will use it.

3. Why we use information

We process information to provide accounts and competition features; maintain scoring, leaderboards and histories; identify authorised organisers; manage purchases and restore access; prevent fraud and protect the service; respond to support requests; and meet legal obligations. Where applicable, our legal bases include performance of a contract, legitimate interests in securing and improving the service, legal obligations, and consent for optional activities where required. Confirm the exact basis for each processing activity before launch.

Competition names, player names, handicaps, scores, photos and results may be visible to other people who have access to a competition, depending on its settings.
