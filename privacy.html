HeadToHeadGolf — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Data controller and operator: Joseph George Meikle, trading as HeadToHeadGolf, 57 Marlborough Road, Lowestoft, Suffolk NR32 3BS, United Kingdom.

Privacy contact: h2hgolf@gmail.com

1. About this policy

HeadToHeadGolf is an app for organising golf competitions, recording match scores, sharing leaderboards and preserving competition histories. This policy explains how we handle personal information about account holders, players entered into competitions by organisers, and spectators. Joseph George Meikle is the controller responsible for personal information processed to operate HeadToHeadGolf. Competition organisers also decide what player information to enter and share for their events.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile information: information used to register and sign in, including your name, email address, account identifier and an optional profile photograph. You do not need to upload a photograph to use the app.

Competition information: competition names and settings, participation and administrative roles, player names, email addresses, handicaps, team assignments, match scores, results, leaderboards, player histories, competition statistics, polls and any photographs submitted for side competitions.

Information supplied by organisers: a competition creator or administrator may enter your name, email address and handicap before you register for HeadToHeadGolf. Email addresses may be used internally to associate your account with the correct player records across competitions. Entering a player email address does not itself trigger an invitation email.

Purchases and support: subscription status and relevant Apple purchase or transaction references used to check creator access, together with information you provide when contacting support. We do not receive your full payment card details from Apple.

Technical information: authentication, service-operation and security records needed to maintain and protect the app. Apple may separately collect app diagnostics under its own privacy arrangements.

3. How and why we use information

We use account information to register and authenticate users, deliver the app and respond to support requests. We use competition records to operate events, calculate scores and handicaps, display results, link player identities and maintain historical statistics. We use purchase information to verify subscription access, and technical records to protect the service and investigate faults or misuse.

Our legal bases under applicable UK data-protection law may include performance of a contract for account and service features, legitimate interests in operating competitions, maintaining accurate sporting histories and protecting the service, and legal obligations where relevant. Where consent is required for a particular optional use, we will seek it. Legitimate interests are considered alongside the privacy rights of affected individuals, including people entered by organisers who have not registered.

4. Who can see competition information

Competitions are not listed in a public directory. People normally access them using a competition joining code. Anyone who obtains a valid code may be able to join the relevant competition, so organisers should share codes carefully.

Players and spectators who join a competition may see player names, optional profile photos, handicaps, teams, scores, leaderboards and historical results. Profile photographs may appear across current and historical competitions linked to the same account. Spectators can view competition information but cannot enter scores or open the live match scoring screen. Scoring and administrative changes are restricted according to the user's role.

Player email addresses are intended to be visible only to the competition creator and authorised administrators, not to ordinary players or spectators.

5. Service providers and sharing

We use Supabase for hosting app data, authentication, database functionality, file storage and server-side operations. We use Apple for App Store distribution and in-app subscription purchasing. These providers process information as needed to deliver their respective services and under their applicable terms and privacy arrangements.

We do not currently use third-party advertising trackers or a dedicated third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app, and we do not sell personal information to advertisers. We may disclose information where legally required or reasonably necessary to protect users, rights or service security.

6. Hosting and international data transfers

Our Supabase project is configured in the London (UK) region. This does not mean that every service-provider operation, support process or backup necessarily remains in the UK. Apple and Supabase may process information in other countries under their applicable arrangements. Where required, international transfers will be supported by an appropriate legal mechanism and safeguards.

7. How long we keep information

We generally keep account information while an account is active and competition records for as long as the relevant competition and its historical results are maintained. This supports ongoing events, accurate score histories and other participants' records.

When a competition creator permanently deletes an entire competition, the service is designed to delete its associated event and competition records. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or be retained where a lawful reason requires it. Deleting a competition does not itself delete other users' accounts.

Retention may differ for security records, support correspondence, purchase-related information and backups. We keep these only as necessary for the relevant operational or legal purpose, subject to applicable retention arrangements.

8. Account deletion and historical records

You can initiate account deletion in the app. If you own competitions or have unresolved ownership transfers, you may first need to transfer or resolve those responsibilities. Eligible requests require confirmation and are then processed by our deletion service.

When deletion is successfully processed, the service removes your authentication account, unlinks your account from competition player records and removes profile photographs stored in your account's profile-photo folder. If processing fails, you may need to contact support to complete or resolve the request.

Deleting your account does not automatically erase historical competition records. Organiser-supplied player names, email addresses, handicaps, scores, results and statistics may remain to preserve competition history where an appropriate lawful basis applies. These records may still identify you and remain subject to applicable privacy rights. You can ask us to review, correct or erase such information using the contact details above.

Information may also remain temporarily in backups or be retained where necessary for legal compliance, security or dispute resolution.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access to personal information, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or an objection to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. Rights are not absolute and some requests may be subject to lawful exceptions.

These rights also apply where a competition organiser entered your information before you created an account. Contact h2hgolf@gmail.com with sufficient details for us to locate the relevant records. We may need to verify your identity. UK residents can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO); people elsewhere may have rights to complain to their local regulator.

10. Age requirements and children

HeadToHeadGolf is intended for adults aged 18 and over. You must be at least 18 to register, access competitions as a player or spectator, organise competitions or participate in a competition. Organisers must enter only adult participants, including players who have not yet registered.

At signup, account holders declare that they are 18 or over. This is a declaration, not independent age verification. We do not routinely collect dates of birth or identity documents. If we learn that information relating to someone under 18 has been entered contrary to these rules, we will review it and take appropriate steps, which may include restricting an account, correcting records or deleting information. Contact h2hgolf@gmail.com with concerns.

11. Messages and notifications

HeadToHeadGolf does not currently send its own marketing emails, promotional messages or push notifications. Account-related authentication messages may be sent where needed to provide sign-in or account services. Apple may separately send purchase receipts, renewal notices or other App Store communications. If we introduce additional notifications or marketing communications, we will update our practices and obtain any permissions required by law.

12. Security

We use authentication, role-based access restrictions and other safeguards designed to protect personal information. No online service can guarantee complete security. Please report suspected privacy or security issues to h2hgolf@gmail.com.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app and legal requirements change. We will provide notice where required. The latest version will be available at https://m33eex.github.io/headtoheadgolf/privacy.html.
