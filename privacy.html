HeadToHeadGolf — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

HeadToHeadGolf is a golf competition management application for organisers, players and spectators. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, shared, retained and protected when you use the app, participate in a competition or contact us.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

HeadToHeadGolf is operated by Joseph George Meikle, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for personal information processed to operate the HeadToHeadGolf service.

Competition organisers also decide which players to include in their competitions and what information to submit. Depending on their circumstances, they may have their own data protection responsibilities.

2. Information we collect

Account information: Your name, email address, account identifier, authentication information and optional profile photograph. Authentication credentials are handled through Supabase authentication services.

Competition information: Competition and team names, membership and roles, player names, email addresses, golf handicaps, match assignments, hole scores, results, leaderboards, historical performances and other competition-related information.

Photographs: Optional account profile photographs and photographs submitted for competition features where available. A profile photograph associated with an account may appear across linked competitions, including historical events.

Subscription information: Subscription entitlement and transaction-related identifiers necessary to manage access to paid features. Apple handles App Store payments; we do not receive your full payment-card details.

Support and security information: Information you provide when contacting us and technical records reasonably needed to operate, troubleshoot and protect the service.

3. Information supplied by competition organisers

Organisers can add players to a competition by entering their first name, last name, email address and handicap. A player does not need to have a registered HeadToHeadGolf account for an organiser to create their competition record.

Although the app checks email formatting and duplicate addresses within a roster, an organiser-entered email address is not independently verified as belonging to that player. Organisers should provide accurate information and ensure participants receive appropriate privacy information.

If a registered user joins a competition, the app may match their account email address to an organiser-entered player record and link the account to that record. Registered users are required to confirm their email address as part of the current signup process.

If an organiser has entered your information and you do not have an account, you can contact us at h2hgolf@gmail.com to ask about your information or exercise applicable privacy rights.

4. How we use information and our legal bases

We use account information to register and authenticate users, provide access to competitions and deliver features requested by users. Where applicable, this is necessary to perform our contract with registered users.

We use competition records to organise events, enable scoring, display leaderboards, preserve results and maintain historical golf records. For information about non-registered participants, and for appropriate historical retention, we may rely on legitimate interests in operating competitions and maintaining accurate sporting records, subject to balancing those interests against individuals' rights, especially those of children.

We use subscription-related information to provide purchased features and meet applicable legal obligations. We use security records to protect accounts, prevent misuse and maintain the reliability of the service, generally on the basis of legitimate interests or legal obligations as applicable.

Where consent is required for a particular activity, we will seek it. Providing an optional photograph does not, by itself, remove your data protection rights or establish consent for every possible use.

5. Who can see your information?

HeadToHeadGolf competitions are not publicly browsable through the app. Access is normally obtained by signing in and entering a competition joining code, or through relevant competition membership arrangements.

People who gain access to a competition may see player names, teams, handicaps, profile photographs where linked, scores, results, leaderboards and competition history. A joining code can be shared or forwarded, so it should not be treated as a guarantee that every viewer is personally known to the organiser.

Player email addresses are intended for competition creators and authorised administrators, rather than ordinary players or spectators. Competition creators and administrators can manage relevant event information according to their permissions. Spectators can view permitted competition information but cannot enter live match scores as players.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers and do not currently use advertising trackers or dedicated third-party analytics SDKs in the app.

6. Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication, databases, storage and related application infrastructure. We use Apple for App Store distribution, in-app purchases and subscriptions. Our public website is hosted using GitHub Pages, which may process technical information when visitors access the website.

These providers may process information under their own applicable privacy notices and contractual arrangements. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect legal rights or security, or in connection with a lawful change in the operation of the service.

7. Where information is processed

The primary Supabase project for HeadToHeadGolf is hosted in the London (eu-west-2) region. This does not mean all associated processing, support operations, backups or service-provider activity necessarily takes place exclusively in the United Kingdom.

HeadToHeadGolf may be accessed internationally. Where personal information is transferred outside the United Kingdom or another jurisdiction requiring transfer safeguards, applicable legal transfer mechanisms and protections must be used.

8. How long information is kept

We retain account information while an account remains active and as otherwise necessary for legitimate operational, security and legal purposes.

Competition records may remain for the lifetime of a competition, including after an event is completed, so that organisers and participants can view results and historical records. These records can include player names, handicaps, scores and other information originally supplied by organisers.

Where a competition is deleted, associated competition information is intended to be removed through the applicable deletion process, subject to technical dependencies, backups and lawful retention requirements.

Information in service-provider backups and security records may remain for a limited period according to applicable operational retention arrangements. Specific backup and diagnostic retention periods are not currently stated as fixed periods in this policy.

9. Account deletion

Registered users can request account deletion through the app, subject to checks needed to protect competitions, resolve event ownership and preserve necessary records. For example, an organiser may need to transfer or resolve ownership of competitions before their account can be deleted.

Once a deletion request is successfully processed, the account is deleted, linked account identifiers are removed from relevant competition records, and the account's stored profile photographs are removed through the deletion process.

Deleting an account does not automatically erase every competition record in which that person has appeared. Organiser-entered names, email addresses, handicaps, scores and historical results may remain where there is a valid lawful basis to retain them. Such information remains personal information where it can identify someone.

You may separately request correction, deletion or restriction of retained personal information. We will assess those requests under applicable law, including the rights and interests of other competition participants.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights to access your personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, and receive certain information in a portable format. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting processing already lawfully carried out.

To exercise your rights, including if you are listed as a player without having an account, email h2hgolf@gmail.com. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity and protect other people's information. We will respond within applicable legal time limits.

If you are in the United Kingdom, you can raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). People in other jurisdictions may have additional rights and complaint routes under their local laws.

11. Children and younger golfers

HeadToHeadGolf accounts are intended for people aged 13 or older. Users purchasing creator subscriptions must be at least 18 years old or the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction. The app does not currently collect dates of birth or independently verify users' ages.

Organisers may include younger golfers in competition records even if those golfers do not have HeadToHeadGolf accounts. Organisers should take particular care when entering information about children, ensure an appropriate lawful basis exists, obtain parental involvement or permission where required, and explain the competition's information-sharing arrangements.

Names, photographs and results relating to younger golfers may be visible to people who have joined the relevant competition. Parents or guardians can contact us to raise concerns or request that a child's personal information be reviewed, corrected or removed where applicable.

We do not knowingly design the service to encourage children to disclose unnecessary personal information. We review privacy risks affecting younger users and may apply additional restrictions where appropriate.

12. Security

We use authentication, access controls and other technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information. Email confirmation is enabled for new account registration. Access to competition data is controlled through application and database permissions.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe information has been accessed improperly or have identified a security concern, please contact h2hgolf@gmail.com.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service develops or legal requirements change. The latest version will be available at https://m33eex.github.io/headtoheadgolf/privacy.html. We will provide additional notice where required by law.
