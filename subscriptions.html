HeadToHeadGolf — Creator Subscription Terms

Draft for review — not yet published

Effective date: [PUBLICATION DATE]

What you buy

The HeadToHeadGolf Creator subscription enables eligible organiser features. Players and spectators can access supported free features without subscribing. The subscription is associated with the purchasing Apple account and the linked HeadToHeadGolf account, subject to successful server verification.

Price and renewal

The intended UK price is £4.99 per year. Prices in other countries are shown in the App Store purchase sheet and may differ by region, tax and Apple's price tiers. This is an auto-renewing annual subscription. Apple charges the applicable price at purchase and on renewal unless you cancel in time under Apple's subscription terms. The App Store purchase screen is the authoritative price and billing disclosure.

Manage or cancel

On an Apple device, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → HeadToHeadGolf to manage or cancel. You can also manage subscriptions through your Apple account. Deleting the HeadToHeadGolf app or account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Cancellation normally stops future renewal; paid access generally continues until the current paid period ends, subject to Apple's policies.

Restore purchases

Use the app's Restore Purchases action while signed in to the appropriate HeadToHeadGolf account. Apple transactions must be verified by our server before creator access is updated. Contact h2hgolf@gmail.com if a valid purchase is not recognised.

Failed renewal, grace and competition scoring

Where Apple's billing grace period applies, eligible Creator access may continue during that period. The intended grace period is 16 days, subject to final App Store configuration and Apple's actual transaction status. If access expires outside an applicable grace period, competition scoring is intended to lock at 8:00 p.m. in the event's configured timezone on the effective expiry date, unless the subscription is renewed or an eligible subscribed member accepts ownership. Existing historical scores are not deleted; unsent scores may remain queued on the device until uploads are authorised again. This behaviour is not yet enabled in production and must be validated before launch.

Refunds and rights

Purchases and refund requests are handled through Apple, subject to applicable consumer law. Visit https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ for Apple's refund request process. Nothing in these terms limits mandatory statutory rights.
