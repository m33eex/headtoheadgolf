HeadToHeadGolf — Subscription Terms

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Free access and HeadToHeadGolf Unlimited

HeadToHeadGolf provides each eligible account with its first 10 completed Solo golf rounds free of charge. Once that allowance is used, an active HeadToHeadGolf Unlimited subscription is required to start further Solo rounds. Previously completed rounds and their scorecards remain available, subject to the app’s account and data-retention terms.

Competition organisers receive their first successfully published event permanently free. Draft events do not consume this allowance. Publishing additional events requires an active HeadToHeadGolf Unlimited subscription unless the account has an applicable Founder entitlement. Joining competitions and participating as a player or spectator is free.

HeadToHeadGolf Unlimited is one auto-renewable annual Apple in-app subscription covering both unlimited additional Solo rounds and eligible additional competition events. The UK price is £24.99 per year. Apple displays the actual price, currency, subscription period and payment terms before confirmation.

2. Renewal and cancellation

Unless cancelled, the subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period, subject to Apple's billing terms and applicable law. You can manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app or your HeadToHeadGolf account does not cancel the Apple subscription.

Cancellation normally prevents future renewals. Paid access generally remains available until the applicable expiry time, subject to Apple's transaction status and any applicable grace period or legal requirements.

3. Payment, refunds and support

Apple processes subscription purchases and payment details. For refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com or Apple Support. For problems with HeadToHeadGolf access, contact h2hgolf@gmail.com. Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

4. Access when a subscription expires

After a paid subscription expires and any applicable grace period ends, an account that has used its 10 free Solo rounds cannot start additional Solo rounds until eligible subscription access is restored. Previously completed Solo rounds and their scorecards are not deleted merely because the subscription expires.

For an organiser’s existing paid published or live competition events, scoring access may become unavailable from 8:00 pm in the event’s local timezone, but never before the actual Apple subscription expiry time. Renewing a valid subscription restores eligible access after verification. The organiser’s permanently free first published event and applicable Founder entitlements are not subject to the paid-event expiry restriction.

5. Availability and changes

Purchasing depends on Apple’s availability for your storefront and the app release. Features and eligibility are subject to these terms and applicable law. Current purchase terms are displayed in the app before payment.
