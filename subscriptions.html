HeadToHeadGolf — Subscription Terms

Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Creator subscription

HeadToHeadGolf offers organisers their first successfully published event free of charge. Draft events do not use this free allowance. After the first free published event, organisers need an active auto-renewing Creator subscription to publish additional events, unless they have a lifetime Founder entitlement. The intended UK Creator subscription price is £24.99 per year, billed through Apple in-app purchases. The actual price, billing currency and subscription period shown by Apple before confirmation control.

2. Renewal and cancellation

Unless cancelled, an auto-renewing subscription renews at the end of each billing period at the then-applicable price, subject to Apple’s notices and applicable law. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app or your HeadToHeadGolf account does not itself cancel an Apple subscription.

Cancellation normally stops future renewals; access generally continues until the end of the paid period unless Apple or applicable law provides otherwise.

3. Payment, refunds and support

Apple handles billing and payment details. For Apple purchase refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com or Apple Support. For app access issues, contact h2hgolf@gmail.com. Statutory rights remain unaffected.

4. Access when a subscription expires

When an organiser’s paid subscription expires, scoring for their existing paid published or live events may become unavailable from 8:00 pm in the event’s local timezone on the expiry date, but never before the actual Apple subscription expiry time. Renewing a valid subscription restores eligible scoring access after the renewal is verified. The organiser’s permanently free first published event and Founder entitlements are not subject to this paid-event expiry restriction.

5. Availability

Subscriptions are available through the app when Apple enables purchasing for the applicable storefront and app release. The app will display current purchase terms before any charge.
